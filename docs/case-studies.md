# Interview case studies

- **Analytics engineering:** Explain immutable cutoffs and member-month grain. Show how late payments change revised paid expense and why missing benchmark values stay visible.
- **Data engineering:** Reproduce an interrupted batch, retry the same manifest, and trace source IDs and watermarks. Compare fresh builds at three population sizes.
- **Applied AI:** Trace a question from allowed intent to parameterized tool result, then compare with independent ledger expectations.
- **AI platform:** Show an authenticated request and why adding a tenant field cannot retarget access. Demonstrate the readiness probe.
- **MLOps:** Describe the versioned batch file and local experiment evidence. Registry and rollback are separate remaining gates.
- **ML engineering:** Explain the as-of features, disjoint holdouts and why a more complex model can perform worse on a different synthetic population.
